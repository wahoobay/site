## Planning Your Visit

### How do I get to Wahoo Bay?

Wahoo Bay is situated within the Hillsboro Inlet at Roy L. Rogers Family Park at 2700 N. Ocean Blvd, Pompano Beach, FL 33062.

### When can I visit Wahoo Bay?

Wahoo Bay is open every day 10 am to 4 pm. For more information on changes in park hours, please visit [Pompano Beach Parks and Recreation](https://parks.pompanobeachfl.gov/wahoo-bay/ways-to-visit-wahoo-bay).

### Is there parking available on site?

Yes! There is limited paid public parking available on site.

### Does Wahoo Bay have lifeguards on site?

Wahoo Bay does not have lifeguards on duty, so it is a swim-at-your-own-risk location. If the water is rough, or you have concerns, we recommend viewing the inlet camera to check water conditions before arriving at the park.

### Can anyone swim in Wahoo Bay whenever they want?

Daily open swim is from 10 am to 4 pm, but is subject to change if there are private events. There is no lifeguard on duty, so swim at your own risk — no scuba diving is allowed. Visit [Pompano Beach Parks and Recreation](https://parks.pompanobeachfl.gov/wahoo-bay/ways-to-visit-wahoo-bay) for private event dates when the park may be closed, as well as other hour updates. Pompano Parks has created ongoing monthly programs that allow the public to access the area safely; marine conservation is our main goal, so access is monitored to protect the health of marine life.

### Does Wahoo Bay provide snorkel equipment?

No, there is no equipment available daily at Wahoo Bay. However, on the 2nd Saturday of every month, Pompano Beach Parks and Recreation hosts an open snorkel for the public with rental gear available.

### What if I can't swim? How can I see the fish?

No matter your age, ability or location, you can view the marine life at Wahoo Bay through our [underwater camera on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@wahoobaypompano/live)!

## Park Rules

### Is diving allowed?

No diving is allowed at Wahoo Bay.

### Can I fish at Wahoo Bay?

No fishing of any kind is allowed in the protected area of Wahoo Bay. You may fish off the north side sea wall in the historic fishing area.

### Are dogs allowed?

No dogs are allowed in the water.

### Can I bring a boat, kayak or paddleboard?

No boats, kayaks, paddleboards or other small craft are allowed in Wahoo Bay. You may launch from the north side of the pier out into the inlet, but not through Wahoo Bay.

### Can I bring a large group?

Groups of 9 or larger are considered organized groups and are not allowed to utilize the park without becoming an approved vendor. For groups over 9, please be sure to become an approved vendor through [Pompano Beach Parks and Recreation](https://parks.pompanobeachfl.gov/wahoo-bay/ways-to-visit-wahoo-bay).

## The Park & Getting Involved

### What are the big structures in the water?

The honey-comb shaped structures you see in the water are called SEAHIVEs. They were developed by researchers at the University of Miami and are built using cost-effective and environmentally-friendly concrete. They act as a shoreline protection system by dispersing wave energy and limiting flooding and erosion while providing habitat for marine life.

### How do I get involved with Wahoo Bay?

We are always looking for passionate community members to become part of our Ambassador team! To learn more, check out our ["Become an Ambassador"](/get-involved/ambassadors/) page.
