Wahoo Bay thrives because of collaboration. From schools and universities to conservation groups and local partners, our work is powered by people who share a vision for healthy oceans and thriving communities. By joining forces, we amplify impact, expand access to education, and build a stronger future for our coastlines and our communities.

## Partnership Impact

### Educational Excellence

Collaborating with leading universities and educational institutions to advance marine science research, provide hands-on learning opportunities for students, and develop innovative curriculum that prepares the next generation of marine conservationists.

### Conservation Leadership

<!-- katie: review: the old text said "to implement cutting-edge restoration techniques"; the site's design check rejects "cutting-edge" as filler, so "cutting-edge" is left out here. Reword if you want the idea back. -->
Working with environmental organizations and marine construction experts to implement restoration techniques, protect critical habitats, and develop sustainable solutions that balance ecological health with community needs.

### Community Engagement

Partnering with government agencies, local chambers of commerce, and community organizations to create policy frameworks, secure funding for conservation projects, and build public support for marine protection initiatives.

## Wahoo Bay & Shipwreck Park

### Research & Collaboration Committee

#### Our Mission

We bring people together—educators, researchers, students, community leaders, and innovators—to co-create projects that benefit both our marine ecosystems and our community.

#### Why Collaborate With Us?

- Supportive partnerships that share resources and expertise
- Access to unique living-lab environments at Wahoo Bay & Shipwreck Park
- Guidance on protocols, permits, and approvals so your project runs smoothly
- Opportunities to showcase and celebrate your work locally and globally

#### How Collaboration Works

#### What We Value

- **Partnership** – working side-by-side with schools, universities, nonprofits, and businesses.
- **Innovation** – supporting creative approaches to research and education.
- **Community Impact** – ensuring projects serve both people and planet. Projects that allow the community to get involved
- **Shared Learning** – celebrating and sharing results broadly
