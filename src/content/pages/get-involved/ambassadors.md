## What is a Wahoo Bay Ambassador?

*Passionate people. Purposeful impact.*

Wahoo Bay Ambassadors are community-minded volunteers of all ages and abilities who help bring marine science, conservation, and education to life along our shoreline reef. From welcoming visitors to supporting student field trips, Ambassadors play a vital role in making the ocean accessible to all.

![Wahoo Bay Ambassadors at parade](/images/get-involved/parade.jpg)

### Our ambassadors have 3 core qualities:

#### Passionate

Wahoo Bay Ambassadors combine passion, curiosity, and service to advance environmental education and conservation throughout South Florida.

#### Community Minded

Our Ambassadors are community-minded individuals who welcome visitors, support programs, and serve as stewards of Wahoo Bay's shoreline reef.

#### Lifelong Learners

As lifelong learners, Wahoo Bay Ambassadors deepen their understanding of marine science while helping others explore, discover, and protect our coastal ecosystems.

### Becoming an Ambassador

All Ambassadors must complete a 3 step process to obtain clearance through the City of Pompano Beach Parks and Recreation Division.

1. Complete the official volunteer application form
2. Submit to required background screening
3. Attend mandatory orientation session
