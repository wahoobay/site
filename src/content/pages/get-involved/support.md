## What Your Support Makes Possible

### Expanding Access

![Students and instructors collecting water samples on the Wahoo Bay dock](/images/about/2024-pxl_20250611_144923455.mp.jpg)

Donor support helps Title I schools participate in classroom programs and field experiences that might otherwise be out of reach.

### Advancing Conservation

![SEAHIVE shoreline structures being installed at Wahoo Bay](/images/about/2023-seahive-installation.jpg)

Researchers, students, and volunteers document biodiversity, monitor conditions, and take part in meaningful conservation activities.

### Developing Tomorrow's Workforce

![Wahoo Bay swim platform installed](/images/about/2021-wahoobay-swim-platform-installed.jpeg)

Students gain exposure to marine science, engineering, data analysis, and conservation careers through mentorship and hands-on projects.

### Building a Model Others Can Use

![Aerial view of the Wahoo Bay site](/WB-1.jpg)

Wahoo Bay brings education, research, technology, and public participation together — a model we share with other communities.
