## Request a Field Trip

Ready to book your field experience? Fill out our request form to get started

### Field Trip Request Form

Complete our online form to request a field trip for your students. We'll work with you to create an unforgettable marine conservation experience tailored to your group's needs and educational objectives.

[Open Request Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfoPfz_0S-LM9DZ0F_Ky5vqbdBTnVVE0S7dEB0DBM07v0lXdQ/viewform) — Google Form • Submit anytime

### Booking Process

1. **Submit Request Form:** Complete our online form with your group details and preferred dates
2. **Confirmation & Planning:** Our team will contact you to confirm details and customize your experience
3. **Pre-Visit Preparation:** Receive planning materials and prepare your students for the experience
4. **Field Experience Day:** Enjoy your immersive marine conservation education experience

### What Information We Need

- School/Organization name
- Grade level and group size
- Preferred visit dates
- Contact information
- Educational objectives
- Special accommodations needed
- Transportation details
- Any additional questions
