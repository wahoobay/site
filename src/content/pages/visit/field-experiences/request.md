## What to Expect in the Form

- **School Information:** Basic details about your school, grade level, and group size
- **Preferred Dates:** Your preferred visit dates and alternative options
- **Learning Objectives:** Curriculum goals and specific learning outcomes you'd like to achieve
- **Contact Details:** Your contact information for scheduling and coordination

Or e-mail these details to [info@wahoobay.org](mailto:info@wahoobay.org?subject=Field%20trip%20request).

## Before You Submit Your Request

- **Review Our Field Trip Guide:** Familiarize yourself with our programs and planning information
- **Watch the Field Experience Video:** See what students can expect during their visit to Wahoo Bay
- **Check Your Calendar:** Have multiple preferred dates ready for maximum scheduling flexibility
- **Confirm Group Details:** Know your final student count and any special accommodations needed

## What Happens Next?

1. **Form Submission:** Complete and submit your field trip request form
2. **Review & Contact:** Our education team will review your request and contact you within 2-3 business days
3. **Program Planning:** We'll work together to customize the experience for your students
4. **Confirmation & Preparation:** Receive final details and pre-visit materials for your field experience

## Questions About Your Field Trip?

Our education team is here to help you plan the perfect field experience. Don't hesitate to reach out if you need assistance with planning or have special requirements for your group.
