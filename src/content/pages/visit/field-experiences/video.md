## Experience Marine Conservation Education

Take a virtual tour of our field experience program and see how students connect with marine science through interactive, hands-on learning opportunities. Our field experiences combine Art, Conservation, and Education to create memorable learning moments for students of all ages.

## What Makes Our Field Experience Special

### Hands-On Marine Science

Students engage directly with marine ecosystems, learning about conservation through interactive activities and real-world observations.

### Art & Science Integration

Our unique ACE approach combines artistic expression with scientific learning, making marine conservation accessible and inspiring.

### Curriculum-Aligned Learning

<!-- katie: review: "Pre-K through collegiate" disagrees with "grades 3–12" on the Book a Field Experience page and the FAQ's minimum age of 8. -->
Programs designed to complement classroom learning with standards-aligned content for all grade levels from Pre-K through collegiate.

### Expert Facilitation

Led by marine science professionals and experienced educators who bring passion and expertise to every learning experience.

## Field Experience Components

1. **Pre-Visit Preparation:** Teachers receive curriculum materials and preparation guides to maximize learning outcomes
2. **Interactive Marine Activities:** Students participate in hands-on conservation projects and marine science investigations
3. **Artistic Expression:** Creative activities that help students connect emotionally with marine conservation
4. **Post-Visit Resources:** Follow-up materials and activities to extend learning back in the classroom
