One of Wahoo Bay’s major goals is to introduce school aged children to the wonders of our oceans through organized, curated field experiences. These short expeditions will not only allow for an immersive experience at Wahoo Bay but also include short lessons on a variety of related topics.

The first public school field experience occurred on May 28, 2024. A group of energetic 3rd and 4th graders from Pompano Beach Elementary School got an up close and personal look at the SEAHIVES and the variety of fish life inhabiting this micro marine sanctuary.
