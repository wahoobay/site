Coastal communities, like ours, depend on natural barriers, such as, mangroves and coral reefs, to help protect the shoreline and limit the impacts of flooding. The cost-effective, environmentally friendly concrete [SEAHIVE](https://sustain.earth.miami.edu/research/projects/seahive-sustainable-estuarine-marine-revetment/index.html)™ structures you see along the seawall protect our shoreline by dispersing wave energy and limiting flooding impacts.
